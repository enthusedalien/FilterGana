Welcome to FilterGana!

This is the home page of the FilterGana extension. In the learning process, furigana can be extremely useful; however, it can also be detrimental. No one can keep the training wheels on forever, and so this extension aims to remove the furigana from websites such as NHK news easy based on YOUR knowledge. Simply add the words you know to the word list, and the extension will automatically remove the furigana for those words!
